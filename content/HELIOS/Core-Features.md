# Core Features

The Smart Executive Mail system is built on four primary pillars that drive institutional intelligence.

## F1: Proactive Executive Intelligence
The **Behavior Engine** analyzes communication patterns to predict:
- Urgent actions before they become crises.
- Stalled threads and delayed responses.
- Recurring workflows that are due for attention.
- Decision bottlenecks in the approval chain.

## F2: AI Digital Twin
The **Digital Twin** provides a live operational model of the institution, including:
- **Department Health**: Monitoring workload and stress levels.
- **Escalation Risks**: Detecting silent issues before they escalate.
- **Communication Gaps**: Identifying silos and missing links between departments.

## F3: Knowledge Graph
The **Knowledge Graph** maps the "social fabric" of the institution:
- **People & Relationships**: Who talks to whom and about what.
- **Projects & Priorities**: Automatic extraction of ongoing initiatives.
- **Key Coordinators**: Identifying the "glue" people who keep the organization moving.

## F4: Executive Command Center
A natural-language interface that allows you to query your institutional data using simple questions:
- *"Which department is most stressed?"*
- *"Find all discussions about NAAC accreditation."*
- *"What are the current bottlenecks affecting budget approvals?"*

The Command Center uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** powered by [[Getting-Started#System Requirements|Ollama (Llama 3.2)]] to provide grounded, professional answers.
